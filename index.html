Our Story

Community cricket, done properly

Heatherton Cricket Club was founded in 1879 as the Heatherton Freighters, making it one of the oldest cricket clubs in Melbourne. We now compete in Cricket Southern Bayside, Cricket Victoria's premier community competition, and play out of our clubrooms on Ross Street, Heatherton, beside Capital Golf Course.

Our motto, Facile Princeps — "easily the best" — has followed the club through generations of players. Whether you're chasing a premiership, want your kids to learn the game properly, or just fancy a Saturday afternoon with good people and a cold drink after, there's a spot for you here.

Our History

From a paddock in Heatherton to a club with a reputation across the competition — the highlights along the way.

One of Melbourne's oldest cricket clubs
Founded in 1879 as the Heatherton Freighters, Heatherton CC is one of the longest continuously running cricket clubs in Melbourne.
Professional international coaching
Our senior and junior programs are led by coaches with professional and international playing experience.
A true community fixture
Multiple generations of the same families have pulled on the club jumper, across seniors, juniors and women's cricket.
1879
Club founded as the Heatherton Freighters
Heatherton Cricket Club is formed under the name Heatherton Freighters, making it one of Melbourne's oldest cricket clubs still playing today.
1920s
Ross St Ground becomes home
The club settles permanently at Ross St Ground, building its first clubhouse and pavilion.
1950s–60s
A golden era of premierships
The 1st XI claims multiple senior premierships across the district competition, cementing the club's reputation as a genuine force.
1998
Junior pathway program launched
A structured juniors program is introduced, taking players from Under 10s through to senior grade cricket.
2015
Professional coaching staff appointed
The club brings on coaches with professional and international playing backgrounds to lead senior and junior training.
2025
Junior cricket program launched
Heatherton launches its junior cricket program, giving young players a dedicated pathway into the club.
2026
Women's cricket established
Heatherton fields its first Women's XI, opening a new chapter in the club's competition history.
Today
Competing in Cricket Southern Bayside

Beyond Match Day

Function Room Hire

Tucked beside the pitch at Ross Street, our function room opens onto a view most function centres can only fake with a mural — open green outfield, the pavilion's timber lines, and, on a good evening, a sky that turns the whole space gold at last light. It's a room that's hosted premiership presentations and christenings with equal ease: warm, unpretentious, and big enough to hold a crowd without ever feeling like a hall.

Whether you're planning a birthday, an engagement, a corporate end-of-year, or a wake that needs somewhere dignified and quiet, the space flexes to fit — long banquet tables for a sit-down dinner, or cleared floor for something looser. Licensed bar on site, ample parking, and a committee that actually picks up the phone.

FAQs

Got questions? We've got answers

Do I need experience to join?
Not at all. We field teams across every level, from complete beginners in the juniors program to seasoned 1st XI players. Everyone starts somewhere.
How much does membership cost?
Use the "Become a Member" or "Pay Your Yearly Subs" buttons at the top of the page, or get in touch if you have questions about fees.
When does training start?
Training runs Tuesdays and Thursdays from 5:30 PM, from 29 September 2026 through to 15 March 2027, at our Ross St ground.
Can my kids play?
Yes — we run a dedicated juniors program with a structured pathway through to senior grade cricket.
Is there a women's team?
Yes, Heatherton fields a Women's XI competing in Cricket Southern Bayside.
How do I get involved as a sponsor?
Reach out via the Contact section — we'd love to talk about sponsorship opportunities with local businesses.
